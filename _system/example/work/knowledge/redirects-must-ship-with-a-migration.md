# Redirects Must Ship With A Migration

**Summary:** Old links break silently unless redirects go live the same day as the content move
**Updated:** 2026-01-21

## Key Points
- Most traffic to documentation comes from links in chat, tickets, and bookmarks, not from search.
- Nobody reports a broken link; they just stop trusting the docs.
- Build the old-to-new mapping before moving content, not after.

## Details
In a trial move of 20 pages, the pages themselves were fine but every inbound link returned "not found". The mapping was easy to produce from the export while the old site was still up, and much harder afterwards.
