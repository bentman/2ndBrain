# Lesson Learned Template

Use for reusable lessons from projects, mistakes, experiments, or repeated patterns.

```markdown
# Lesson: [Title]

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Context:** [Project, situation, or source]
**Category:** Technical | Process | Planning | Communication | Troubleshooting | Other

## What Happened
[Brief situation]

## Lesson
[Actionable takeaway]

## Why It Matters
[Where this applies again]

## Do Differently Next Time
- [Action]

## Related
- [Link]
```

Store durable lessons in `_user/data/knowledge/`, not buried only in project notes.
