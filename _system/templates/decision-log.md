# Decision Log Template

Use for decisions where a future reader may ask, "Why did we choose this?"

```markdown
# Decision: [Title]

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Status:** Proposed | Decided | Implemented | Reconsidering | Superseded
**Context:** [Project or situation]

## Problem
[What decision is needed and why]

## Options

### Option A: [Name]
- Pros:
- Cons:

### Option B: [Name]
- Pros:
- Cons:

## Decision
[Chosen option]

## Reasoning
[Why this option wins given the constraints]

## Consequences
- Enables:
- Prevents or makes harder:
- Risks:

## Follow-up
- [ ] [Action or review point]

## Related
- [Link]
```

Keep it short. Skip sections that do not apply.
